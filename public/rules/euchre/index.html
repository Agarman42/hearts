How to play Euchre

Hearts Spades Euchre

Partners, a short deck, and a race to 10. This is the kitchen table in Card Parlour. The deck is nines through aces. Settings can turn off stick-the-dealer or play to 5.

Naming trump

  • One card is turned up. In order around the table, order the dealer to pick it up, or pass.
  • If the dealer picks it up, they discard one card. That suit is trump.
  • If everyone passes, the card is turned down. Players then name a different suit.
  • If everyone passes again, the dealer has to name trump.
  • The team that named trump are the makers. They may send their partner out and play alone.

The play

  • The player to the dealer's left leads. A partner who sat out does not play.
  • Follow suit. The jack of the same color as trump is the left bower, and it counts as trump when you follow.
  • The jack of trump, the right bower, is the highest card. Then the left bower, then ace, king, queen, ten, and nine of trump.

The score

  • Makers with 3 or 4 tricks score 1. All 5 tricks is a march and scores 2.
  • Alone, all 5 tricks scores 4. Alone with 3 or 4 tricks still scores 1.
  • Makers with fewer than 3 tricks are euchred. The other team scores 2.
  • The first team to 10 wins.
Play now

Play for fun. The table does not take wagers.
