Card Parlour

How to play Hearts

Hearts Spades Euchre

Four players, one deck, and a race to stay under 100. This is the kitchen table in Card Parlour. Settings can turn the pass off or count the jack of diamonds.

The pass

Deal 13 cards each. Pass three cards to the left, then the next hand to the right, then across, then keep your cards. After that the cycle starts again.

The play

  • The two of clubs leads the first trick.
  • Follow the suit that was led. If you cannot, play anything else that is legal.
  • The highest card of the led suit takes the trick. There is no trump.
  • You cannot lead a heart until a heart has been played, unless hearts are all you have.
  • On the first trick, do not play a heart or the queen of spades if you have another card.

The score

  • Each heart taken is 1 point. The queen of spades is 13.
  • Take every heart and the queen and you shot the moon. You score 0. Each other player scores 26.
  • When any score reaches 100, the match ends. The lowest score wins.
Play now

Play for fun. The table does not take wagers.
