Card Parlour

How to play Spades

Hearts Spades Euchre

Partners sit across the table and race to 500. This is the kitchen table in Card Parlour. Settings can add blind nil or turn the bag penalty off.

The bid

Each player bids how many tricks they will take. You and your partner add those numbers. A nil bid is a promise to take none, and it does not add to the contract.

The play

  • Follow the suit that was led. If you cannot, play any card.
  • Spades are trump. A spade beats any other suit.
  • Do not lead a spade until a spade has been played, unless spades are all you have.

The score

  • Make the contract and the team scores 10 points for each bid trick, plus 1 point for each trick over.
  • Those extra tricks are bags. Every 10 bags costs the team 100 points.
  • Miss the contract and the team loses 10 points for each bid trick. That hand adds no bags.
  • A nil with no tricks scores +100. A nil that takes a trick scores −100.
  • Tricks from both partners count toward the contract, including a nil bidder.
  • The first team to 500 wins.
Play now

Play for fun. The table does not take wagers.
